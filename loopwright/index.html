How an engagement works

No platform to learn and no six-month project. You get one working automation within two weeks, then we expand from there.

  1. Workflow audit

    A 45-minute call and a look at how work moves through your tools. We pick the one workflow with the clearest payback.

  2. Build & test

    We build the automation against your real data, with a human approval step wherever money or customers are involved.

  3. Run & improve

Questions

Anything else, ask us when your pilot slot opens.

Do we need technical staff to use this?

No. We build, host and maintain everything. Your team keeps using the tools they already use; the work simply shows up done, or ready to approve.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every workflow has confidence checks. Anything uncertain goes to a person for review instead of being processed automatically, and anything involving payments or customer messages needs a human approval by default.

Where does our data go?

Data stays in your existing tools and is processed only to run your workflows. We don't use your data to train models, and we sign an NDA and DPA before we touch anything.

What does it cost?

Pilot clients get their first workflow built free. After that, pricing is a flat monthly fee per workflow, quoted up front after the audit.
